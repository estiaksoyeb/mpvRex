mpvRex is a feature-rich, open-source video player for Android based on libmpv. It combines the powerful playback engine of mpv with a modern, customizable Jetpack Compose interface.

Features:

• Playback Engine: High-performance video decoding via libmpv, hardware acceleration, and HDR-to-SDR tone mapping.
• Intuitive Gestures: Circular double-tap seek, subtitle drag-to-reposition, gesture seek cancellation, and precision frame-by-frame controls.
• Modern UI: Sleek glassmorphism interface, dynamic accent color matching, and customizable tab layouts.
• Media & Network Explorer: Unified file browser supporting local storage, network shares (SMB, WebDAV, FTP), M3U playlists, and media library views.
• Advanced Customization: Saved per-video aspect ratio, zoom, pan, orientation, and track memory.

Privacy & Freedom:

• Fully open source (Apache 2.0)
• Zero trackers or analytics
• No advertisements
